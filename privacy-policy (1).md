# ReelMeter Privacy Policy

Last updated: [02/11/2026]

ReelMeter ("the app") is an Android app published by [AshMade] ("we", "us"). It counts the Instagram Reels you scroll through each day and shows you the number. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: everything stays on your phone, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Contact: [appsbyashtech@gmail.com]

## The short version

- ReelMeter has no internet permission, so it cannot upload or send anything by itself.
- No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services.
- We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.
- The app is not included in Android's cloud backup.
- You can erase your data inside the app at any time, or by uninstalling.

## How the app uses Android's Accessibility service

ReelMeter uses Android's Accessibility service for one purpose only: to notice when you swipe to the next reel in Instagram, so it can count it and show the floating counter. It is not used for anything else.

While Instagram is open, Android tells the app:
- which screen is showing,
- the technical names and positions of the on-screen elements that are visible, and
- when the Reels feed scrolls.

The app does not read the text on your screen. It does not read your messages, comments, posts, usernames or passwords, and it does not take screenshots or record your screen. Android also reports when you open other apps. The app ignores those events, except to hide the floating counter when you leave Instagram.

The Accessibility service is off until you switch it on yourself in Android settings, after agreeing to an in-app notice that explains the above. You can switch it off at any time in Android's Accessibility settings. If you switch it off, the app asks for your agreement again the next time you enable the counter.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following on your phone, in its private storage. We cannot see it.

- Your daily reel counts (a date and a number).
- How long the Reels screen was open each day, to show your time spent. If you stop swiping for more than three minutes, the extra time is not counted.
- A few timestamps, such as when Instagram was last seen and when the last reel was counted. These power the "Not counting?" help screen.
- Your settings, such as whether milestone messages are on.
- Your record of agreeing to the Accessibility notice.
- If you choose one, a custom mascot picture. It is copied into the app's private storage and made smaller. The app can only access the single picture you pick.

## Information we receive

We receive nothing automatically. The only exception is the optional "Email report to developer" button in the Help tab. If you tap it, the app opens your own email app with a report already written. The report contains your phone's manufacturer and model, your Android version, your Instagram version, whether the service is on, the timing information described above, and technical names of Instagram screen elements. It contains no personal content, and nothing is sent unless you press Send in your email app. If you send it, we use it only to fix counting problems. You can read and edit the report before sending.

## Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone. The app contains no advertising or analytics code and no third-party SDKs.

## Backup and transfer

ReelMeter is excluded from Android's cloud backup. If you reinstall the app or move to a new phone, your counts and settings will not be restored.

## Your choices and deleting your data

- Switch the Accessibility service off at any time in Android's Accessibility settings.
- Tap "Reset all counts" in the Help tab to erase your saved counts and history.
- Uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your phone.

## Children

ReelMeter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.
