# ReelMeter Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

ReelMeter ("the app") is an Android app published by [AshMade] ("we", "us"). It counts the Instagram Reels you scroll through each day, measures how long you spend on them, and shows you the numbers. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: everything the app measures stays on your phone, and we receive nothing from the app.

Contact: [appsbyashtech@gmail.com]

## The short version

- No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no server of ours. ReelMeter's own code makes no network requests.
- Your counts, your time on Reels, your settings and your pictures stay on your phone. We cannot see them.
- We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.
- The only outside party involved is Google Play, which processes the optional one-time purchase (see "Purchases").
- The app is not included in Android's cloud backup.
- You can erase your data inside the app at any time, or by uninstalling.

## How the app uses Android's Accessibility service

ReelMeter uses Android's Accessibility service for one purpose only: to notice when you swipe to the next reel in Instagram, so it can count it, measure how long the Reels screen is open, and show the floating counter. It is not used for anything else.

While Instagram is open, Android tells the app:
- which screen is showing,
- the technical names and positions of the on-screen elements that are visible, and
- when the Reels feed scrolls.

The app does not read the text on your screen. It does not read your messages, comments, posts, usernames or passwords, and it does not take screenshots or record your screen. Android also reports when you open other apps. The app ignores those events, except to hide the floating counter when you leave Instagram.

The Accessibility service is off until you switch it on yourself in Android settings, after agreeing to an in-app notice that explains the above. You can switch it off at any time in Android's Accessibility settings. If you switch it off, the app asks for your agreement again the next time you enable the counter.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following on your phone, in its private storage. We cannot see it.

- Your daily reel counts (a date and a number).
- How long the Reels screen was open each day, to show your time spent. If you stop swiping for more than three minutes, the extra time is not counted.
- A few timestamps, such as when Instagram was last seen and when the last reel was counted. These power the "Not counting?" help section.
- Your settings, such as whether milestone messages are on.
- Your record of agreeing to the Accessibility notice.
- Whether the optional custom-mascot feature is unlocked. This is a yes-or-no copy of what Google Play last told the app.
- If you unlock the custom-mascot feature and choose a picture, a copy of that picture. The app shrinks it, and if you choose, removes a plain background from it. All of this happens on your phone and the picture is never uploaded. The app can only access the single picture you pick.

## Purchases

The custom-mascot feature is an optional one-time purchase. It is sold and processed entirely by Google Play.

- We never see your payment details, name, email address or Google Account. Google handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy.
- Your purchase belongs to your Google Account. When the app opens, it asks Google Play whether your account owns the feature. If you reinstall the app or move to a new phone and sign in to the same Google Account, the feature is restored automatically. If Google refunds the purchase, the feature locks again.
- ReelMeter has no accounts of its own, so a purchase cannot be restored without Google Play.

## Permissions and network access

- **Accessibility service**: described above. You turn it on yourself.
- **Google Play purchase permission**: lets the app start and check the optional purchase.
- **Internet and network state**: ReelMeter's own code does not use the Internet. These permissions are included with Google's Play Billing library, which the app contains to sell the optional purchase. Google's library may exchange technical and purchase-related information with Google, as described in Google's privacy policy. We do not receive any of it.

## Information we receive

We receive nothing automatically. The only exception is the optional "Email report to developer" button in the Help tab. If you tap it, the app opens your own email app with a report already written. The report contains your phone's manufacturer and model, your Android version, your Instagram version, whether the service is on, the timing information described above, and technical names of Instagram screen elements. It contains no personal content, and nothing is sent unless you press Send in your email app. If you send it, we use it only to fix counting problems. You can read and edit the report before sending.

## Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone. The app contains no advertising or analytics code. Its only third-party component is Google's Play Billing library, used for the optional purchase as described above.

## Backup and transfer

ReelMeter is excluded from Android's cloud backup. If you reinstall the app or move to a new phone, your counts, time and settings are not restored, and neither is a custom mascot picture. The optional purchase is restored by Google Play if you use the same Google Account.

## Your choices and deleting your data

- Switch the Accessibility service off at any time in Android's Accessibility settings.
- Tap "Reset all counts" in the Help tab to erase your saved counts, time and history.
- Tap "Use default" in the Premium tab to remove your custom mascot picture.
- Uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your phone.

We keep nothing on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.
